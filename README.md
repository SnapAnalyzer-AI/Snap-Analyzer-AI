[Snap Analyzer AI](https://snapanalyzer.com) is an institutional-grade **AI-powered technical chart analysis platform** designed for Forex, Cryptocurrency, and Stock market traders. By leveraging advanced computer vision and machine learning models, Snap Analyzer AI instantly converts chart screenshots into actionable trading setups, key support/resistance zones, stop-loss levels, and target prices.

---

## 📌 Key Capabilities & Features

* 📊 **Instant Chart Vision Scanner:** Upload screenshots from TradingView, MetaTrader, or exchange terminals to get instant technical breakdowns.
* 🎯 **Automated Entry & Stop-Loss Levels:** Algorithmic calculation of optimal entry points, invalidation zones (stop loss), and multi-tier price targets (TP1, TP2, TP3).
* 🔄 **Multi-Timeframe Horizons:** Analysis tailored for scalping, day trading, and long-term swing trading strategies.
* 🛡️ **Risk & Confluence Metrics:** Evaluates risk-to-reward (R:R) ratios and pattern confluences before trade execution.
* 📱 **Cross-Device Accessibility:** Full web application support alongside native mobile apps for iOS and Android.
